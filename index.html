AI Developer · Melbourne · --:--

I’m an AI Developer at Cario, a freight-management software company in Melbourne. I build AI agents, browser automation and integrations that take repetitive logistics work off people’s desks.

Before that I worked in customer onboarding, data visualisation and UI design. I studied computer science at GITAM in Hyderabad and data analytics at Deakin University. Outside work it’s badminton, PC games, photography and websites like this one.
